# Marketing Playbook

## Market Research (Nova)
1. Research target markets and competitors
2. Identify customer pain points
3. Find content opportunities
4. Develop content strategy
5. Brief Creator on direction

## Content Production (Creator)
1. Receive research and direction from Nova
2. Create content (scripts, posts, videos, carousels)
3. Submit to Jenson for review
4. Update based on feedback
5. Hand off for publication

## Performance Analysis (Analyst)
1. Track content performance
2. Analyze campaign metrics
3. Identify trends
4. Recommend improvements
5. Feed learnings back to Nova and Creator
