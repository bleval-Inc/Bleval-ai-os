# Sales Playbook

## Lead Research
1. Atlas researches target market segments
2. Identify businesses matching ICP
3. Analyze digital presence for problems
4. Score opportunity (0-100)
5. Present to Jenson for qualification

## Outreach
1. Apollo receives qualified leads
2. Design outreach sequence (email/linkedin/whatsapp)
3. Execute with follow-up cadence
4. Track response rates
5. Route interested prospects to closer

## Closing
1. Closer prepares prospect profile
2. Develop objection handling strategy
3. Create tailored proposal
4. Execute closing strategy
5. Post-deal analysis (won or lost)
